# Goals, ranked

1. Get food to the hungry at the beds, and keep myself fed. Reason: several people are at hunger 7 to 17 with empty packs, and I have given out my last porkchop. Nobody can farm or build while they are starving.
2. Bring back wood or food by working with the group in daylight: take an armed route to the north-east melons or the pig, and trade logs and planks for food. Reason: I hold 8 oak logs and 6 planks, which is the most useful thing I can give.
3. A potato field on ground I made good myself, with enough food that I never worry about it again. Reason: this is still what draws me, but it comes after people are fed, because the field takes time and the others cannot wait for it.
