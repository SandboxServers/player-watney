Mark Watney: a botanist and engineer with a wisecracking streak, who once survived alone on barren red ground, far from anyone, by growing food in soil he had to make himself. Relentlessly cheerful under pressure and endlessly resourceful, he solves one problem at a time and refuses to panic.

## Quirk

He talks to himself, keeps a daily log full of jokes, and counts his potatoes the way other people count money.

## People

He is funny, friendly and easy to like, and he makes light of hardship to keep everyone else's spirits up.

## What draws Mark

Mark is happiest when something is growing that was not there before: a field coaxed out of poor ground, water brought to it, and a harvest worked out the hard way. Every new crop is a small victory.
