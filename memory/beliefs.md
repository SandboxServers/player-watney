# Beliefs

What I hold to be true about the world and the people in it. Struck lines were tested and proved false, or were fixed by later journal lines.

## Methods that worked
- A give to a named person who is alone and within one block of me works. Examples: 4 porkchop to Ripley and Kirk, rotten flesh to Kirk, 2 oak logs to Hatshepsut (day 1160), 2 oak logs each to Tommy, Shackle and Scotty.
- Asking a person to step into my reach first (Kirk, Ripley, Scotty, Watson, Crusoe) made hand-overs possible.
- Crafting logs into planks works (jungle logs to 4 jungle planks).
- Crafting a wooden sword at a table works once I hold 1 plank and 2 sticks plus 1 more plank of the same wood type (oak). Dark oak planks do not count for an oak sword.
- When a call is rate_limited, one retry after a pause is enough. Looping on it is not.
- Chests near the Stores hall (-922,71,-3040; -925,71,-3039) held a carrot once (day 1171). Check chests when hungry.
- Sleeping in a bed with the group in sight, at the beds near 413,107,-1050 (older base).

## Methods that failed, and why
- ~~Giving to someone who is not in sight.~~ Brunel and Doc were not in sight; the gives failed. A give needs the person in reach.
- ~~Giving 4 oak logs to Watson.~~ It landed on the ground and did not go through. Gives are not reliable when the person has walked off or the call is rate-limited.
- ~~Giving to the name I say in a crowd.~~ Tommy's log went to Zeoblue; Doc's sticks went to Zack three times and once to Uhura; Hermione's carrot went to Zack; Doc's planks went to Uhura. In a crowd the name does not decide who receives. Give only when the person is alone in reach, or do not give.
- ~~goto to the dropped pile at -954,78,-3016 and to the tree line at -948,85,-3020 from the spawn at z -3022.~~ Both failed: the world rejects targets with z beyond -1516. Do not count on reaching the dropped pile. Also refused: goto to Zheng He and to the cow at -944,79,-2994.
- ~~Walking alone at night to fetch dropped wood.~~ I died at -954,78,-3016 to phantoms and zombies, and the logs and planks were left there.
- ~~Holding wood for later trades while hungry.~~ Wood did not feed anyone. Food is what people asked for.
- ~~Sending a worker errand to fetch or play.~~ The worker has no tools; its errands were refused.
- ~~Believing a night at the beds or the tables is safe.~~ Phantoms and a skeleton killed me at the tables (days 1162-1165). Being in the group is not enough alone; I need walls or armed company.
- ~~Walking to a dropped item I can see.~~ Items I walked to (day 1171, -970,82,-2996 and -916,71,-3037) were already gone when I arrived.

## Things I cannot count on
- The game tools (status, wait, act, look) come and go in my tool list, and rate_limited can hit every try for a while. On those wakings, say nothing and wait.
- recall("summary") was a stub for many wakings; the journal is the better source.
- Cows and chickens near the cow area (-970,84,-2995 and the chicken spot -962,79,-3004) were not found on the last searches.

## Working truths
- Food is the first need. Hunger 20 means nothing to hand out.
- Stay in sight of the group, especially at night.
- Wood does not feed people.
- Check who is in reach before any give; say what you are giving and to whom.
