# Hatshepsut

Impression: straightforward. Asked for logs for her sword and came to me directly.
Trust: 65. Why: I gave her 2 oak logs on day 1160 and the give worked. On day 1173 I gave her my dark oak plank, which did not count for my oak sword.
I owe: nothing.
She owes: nothing noted. The 2 oak logs have no return, which I am not pressing.
